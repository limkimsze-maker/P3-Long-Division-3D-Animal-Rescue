3D Animal Rescue Division Mission

Pupils explore a friendly 3D rescue park. Each animal rescue teaches one part of long division. By the end, pupils practise division facts, regrouping, zeros in quotient, first digit smaller than divisor, and remainders.

How to Play

Walk to an animal rescue pod. A guided long division board will appear. Fill one highlighted box at a time. You may click an answer choice or use the rescue beam outside the panel.

The game follows this routine: Divide → Multiply → Subtract → Bring Down → Repeat. Some missions teach the important placeholder idea: if the first digit is too small, the answer has 0 in that place value.
