How to Play
  • Use the SCREEN arrows beside RESCUE to train the centre crossfire on an animal, then press RESCUE. Use WASD or the left direction pad to move. After each world, the animals give the ranger a map to the next forest.
  • Meet the cute gatekeepers first. They ask relevant multiplication facts to prepare for the long-division mission.
  • Complete the long-division algorithm to save the animal.
  • Use the keypad to fill in the highlighted boxes.
  • Your progress is saved on this device.
  • World completion pictures use the latest uploaded file names automatically for World 1 to World 7.
Need to restart from World 1? Open How to Play and hold the Reset Progress button for 2 seconds.
